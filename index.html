A bit about Rosie

A versatile storyteller, Rosie blends technical skill with a passion for capturing genuine, spontaneous moments. From working as a lighting technician at MONA to tutoring at UTAS and photographing weddings and partaking in arts residencies overseas, she thrives in diverse roles. Her work reflects a deep connection to the arts community, and she also engages in public artworks and consultancy, helping others bring their creative visions to life. Rosie approaches every project with enthusiasm and a commitment to storytelling, transforming everyday scenes into something extraordinary.
